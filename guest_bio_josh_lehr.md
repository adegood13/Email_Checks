# Guest Bio: Josh Lehr

**No One Asked Us — Guest Introduction**

Josh Lehr is the Founder of **Lehrjet LLC**, a Lincoln, Nebraska consulting firm that helps mortgage lenders and fintechs with partnership strategy, business development, and M&A.

Before hanging his own shingle, Josh was Senior Director of Partnerships & Industry Technology at **Total Expert**, where he built and launched their Consumer Direct platform — work that landed him on HousingWire's **2021 HW Insiders** list. Before that, he ran industry development at **Mortech** (a Zillow Group company), working with some of the nation's biggest online lenders.

Nearly two decades in financial services, nine-plus in mortgage tech, and a University of Nebraska alum.

Please welcome **Josh Lehr**.
