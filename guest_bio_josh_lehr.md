# Guest Bio: Josh Lehr

**No One Asked Us — Guest Introduction**

Josh Lehr is a mortgage and fintech strategist with nearly two decades of experience in financial services and nine-plus years focused squarely on the mortgage industry. Today he's the Founder of **Lehrjet LLC**, a strategic consulting firm based in Lincoln, Nebraska that helps mortgage companies, lenders, and financial services organizations with partnership strategy, business development, M&A support, and go-to-market execution.

Before launching Lehrjet, Josh spent several years at **Total Expert**, where he joined in 2020 as Director of Strategy, Consumer Direct, and rose to Senior Director of Partnerships & Industry Technology. At Total Expert he developed and launched Total Expert for Consumer Direct — a data-driven platform helping lenders better connect with borrowers through marketing automation — and drove more than 30% of the product's anticipated revenue goals in its first half-year. That work earned him a spot on HousingWire's **2021 HW Insiders** list, which recognizes the operational all-stars behind the biggest names in housing.

Prior to Total Expert, Josh was Industry Development Manager at **Mortech** (a Zillow Group business), where he partnered with some of the nation's top online lenders and lead sources to grow Mortech and Zillow's online lending product suite, overseeing API implementations, compliance, product management, and partner strategy.

Josh is a graduate of the **University of Nebraska-Lincoln** (2001–2005) and has built a reputation across the mortgage tech world as one of the most trusted advisors on consumer-direct lending, partnerships, and how technology is reshaping the way borrowers and lenders connect.

Please welcome to *No One Asked Us* — **Josh Lehr**.
