## Sitewide notes

- **One primary CTA everywhere:** "Book a demo." Use "See how it works" or
  "Watch a 2-min demo" as the only secondary CTA.
- **Retire "#1" and "for any department"** as headline claims.
- **Use "instant answers" sparingly** — once per page, then vary the phrasing.
- **Replace abstract jargon** ("multi-agent orchestration," "answer-first,"
  "defensible") with plain outcomes, except where a buyer specifically needs the
  technical reassurance (security section).
- **Fix dash typography:** use em-dashes with consistent spacing; no stray
  hyphens standing in for em-dashes.
- **Add proof** wherever a `[bracket]` placeholder appears — logos, metrics,
  testimonials. This is the highest-impact addition to the current site.
- **Brand clarity:** keep branding consistently "AskBobAI" to separate from the
  unrelated askbobai.org product.
